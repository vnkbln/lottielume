# Centerline v0.4 revisions

23 files reworked; names and paths preserved. The remaining 107 files are unchanged. Quiet_Depth and Ring_Current are retained. Peripheral_Rays is in BG/Lines.

Reworked backgrounds use continuous motion with individual phases and invisible wrapping. Peak opacity is 100%, without an overall dimming multiplier. Four revised Shapes use uniform X/Y scale.

Validation used native ThorVG for all 130 files and a separate 9:16 Fill review of 19 revised backgrounds at 192 sample frames over 8 seconds. Each of those 19 had visible content in every sampled frame. This measures sampled visibility, not artistic quality or visibility at every possible time/aspect.
